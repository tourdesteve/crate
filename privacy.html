Privacy

Binflip has no server and no accounts of its own. Everything it knows about you stays in your browser on your device.

In short: no tracking, no ads, no analytics, no cookies. Binflip talks directly from your device to Discogs and, if you choose, to Spotify. Nobody else, including the person who made Binflip, can see your data.

What Binflip keeps on your device

  • Your Discogs username, and your Discogs personal access token if you add one.
  • A copy of your collection, so Binflip opens quickly. It’s refreshed from Discogs at least every 6 hours.
  • Recently viewed album covers, kept for up to 6 hours so scrolling is smooth.
  • Your settings: the music service you picked, your view, sort and filters.
  • Your listening log: which records you played from Binflip and when.
  • If you connect Spotify: your Spotify Client ID, sign-in tokens, the speaker you picked, and which Spotify album matches each of your records.

This is stored in your browser’s local storage and never leaves your device except as described below. Your phone and computer each keep their own copy.

Who Binflip talks to

  • Discogs, to read your collection, tracklists, grading, notes and collection value. Requests go straight from your device to Discogs using your username and, if you added one, your token. See the Discogs privacy policy.
  • Spotify, only if you connect it, to find albums and control playback. Sign-in happens on Spotify’s own page. See the Spotify privacy policy.
  • Your music service (Apple Music, YouTube Music, TIDAL, Deezer or Amazon Music), only when you tap Open. Binflip sends that app the artist and album name to search for.
  • GitHub Pages serves Binflip’s own files, and Google Fonts serves its typefaces. Like any website, they can see your IP address when your browser downloads those files.

Deleting your data

  • In Binflip, open Settings → Switch account to remove your Discogs details and collection copy, or Spotify settings → Disconnect to remove your Spotify sign-in.
  • To remove everything, clear this site’s data in your browser settings, or delete Binflip from your Home Screen.
  • You can revoke your Discogs token at discogs.com/settings/developers and remove Binflip’s Spotify access at spotify.com/account/apps.
